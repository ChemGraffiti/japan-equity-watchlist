## 注釈 / Annotations
- **PER (株価収益率)**: 株価が1株当たり利益の何倍かを示す指標。
- **PBR (株価純資産倍率)**: 株価が1株当たり純資産の何倍かを示す指標。1倍未満は理論上「解散価値」を下回る。
- **信用倍率**: 信用取引の買い残高÷売り残高。高いほど将来の売り圧力（利益確定・返済売り）が潜在的に大きいとされる。
- **決算発表**: 上場企業が四半期・通期の業績を公表するイベント。株価が大きく動く材料になりやすい。

## Annotations (EN)
- **PER (Price-to-Earnings Ratio)**: Share price divided by earnings per share.
- **PBR (Price-to-Book Ratio)**: Share price divided by net assets per share; below 1x theoretically means trading below liquidation value.
- **Margin/credit ratio (信用倍率)**: Margin buy balance divided by margin sell balance; a higher ratio implies more latent future selling pressure (profit-taking/repayment sales).
- **Earnings announcement (決算発表)**: A listed company's quarterly/annual results release — a common share-price catalyst.
