韓国KOSPIも米国株高を追い風に反発していますが、個人・外国人投資家が売り越しという需給構成は、上昇の持続性については楽観しきれない材料だと考えます（推測）。本日は住友金属鉱山・楽天グループの決算発表を控えており、後場から翌営業日にかけて個別銘柄レベルの値動きがより重要になる可能性があると見ています（推測）。8/12の米CPI発表までは、本日のような強い上昇が続くか、いったん材料出尽くしで一服するかは見極めが必要と考えます（推測）。

*This is Claude's own reasoning/forecast, not a guarantee. This morning's Tokyo rally appears to closely track the overnight US semiconductor-led rally, and the gap between the Nikkei's +2.02% and TOPIX's +0.79% suggests the buying is concentrated in chip names rather than broad-based (subjective). Related sectors — data-center/optical (Fujikura), IT/cyber, and domestic consumer names — also appear to be participating, while autos/transport lagged, roughly flat to slightly down despite the index-wide strength; a modestly stronger yen (upper-157 range) may be a partial headwind for export-sensitive autos, though this is speculative and unconfirmed. Korea's KOSPI is also higher on the US read-through, but a retail/foreign net-selling, institution net-buying mix suggests the rally's durability isn't a given (subjective). With Sumitomo Metal Mining and Rakuten both reporting earnings today, individual stock reactions may matter more than the index level into the afternoon and tomorrow. Whether today's strength persists or fades on profit-taking ahead of the 8/12 US CPI print remains to be seen (subjective).*
